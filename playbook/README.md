# Community-Led Growth Playbook for B2B SaaS

## Objective

This playbook provides a practical framework for building and scaling a community-led growth strategy for B2B SaaS companies. It combines insights gathered from industry experts, research articles, LinkedIn posts, and YouTube discussions, while also including my own analysis and recommendations.

The objective is to help startups and growth teams build engaged communities that contribute to customer acquisition, activation, retention, and advocacy.

## Who This Playbook Is For

This playbook is intended for:
- Early-stage and growth-stage B2B SaaS startups
- Community Managers
- Growth Marketers
- Founder-led startups
- Marketing and Product teams looking to build community as a growth channel

> **Note:** This playbook is based on research conducted in July–August 2026 and reflects the author's evaluation of multiple industry perspectives rather than the opinion of a single expert.
